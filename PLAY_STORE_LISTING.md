# Play Store Listing

## App Name
Studetra

## Short Description
Stay organized with your classes, timetable, and assignments in one simple app.

## Long Description
Studetra is a simple and practical app designed to help students stay organized throughout the semester. Add your units, lecturers, venues, class times, and assignments in one place, then view everything in a clean weekly timetable layout.

Whether you are managing a busy study schedule or trying to stay on top of deadlines, this app makes it easier to plan your week and keep track of important academic tasks. You can quickly see your classes, monitor pending assignments, and stay focused without juggling multiple tools.

Built with simplicity in mind, Studetra helps you organize your academic life with confidence and clarity. Stay prepared, stay on track, and never miss an important class or deadline again.
